Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how AquaLog ("the App"), developed by Onour Impram (operating under the brand name Hezarfen), handles your information. We are committed to protecting your privacy and being transparent about our data practices.

AquaLog is designed with a minimal-data approach. The vast majority of app functionality works entirely on your device without collecting or transmitting any personal information.

Information We Collect

The App collects minimal data. Specifically:

  • App usage data (anonymous): General, non-identifying analytics about how the app is used (e.g., session length, feature usage). This data cannot be linked to any individual user.
  • Ad identifiers (anonymous): If the App displays advertisements via Google AdMob, anonymous advertising identifiers may be used to serve relevant ads. These identifiers are not linked to your name, email address, or any other personally identifiable information.

We do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, location, photos, contacts, or any other personal data.

Local Data Storage

All app data generated by your use of AquaLog (such as your game progress, settings, logs, or records) is stored locally on your device only. This data never leaves your device and is not transmitted to our servers or any third party.

You can delete all locally stored data at any time by uninstalling the App from your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

AquaLog may display advertisements served by Google AdMob, a service provided by Google LLC. AdMob may use anonymous advertising identifiers (such as the Android Advertising ID or Apple Identifier for Advertisers) to serve ads.

Google's use of advertising identifiers is governed by Google's own Privacy Policy, available at policies.google.com/privacy. You can opt out of personalised advertising in your device settings at any time.

Analytics

The App may use anonymous analytics tools to understand how the App is used in aggregate. Analytics data is fully anonymised and cannot be used to identify you personally. This data helps us improve the App experience for all users.

Analytics data collected includes information such as: app version, device type (in generalised form), session duration, and feature interaction counts. No personally identifiable information is included.

Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your personal data with third parties.

The only third-party integrations in AquaLog are:

  • Google AdMob (anonymous ad serving, as described above)
  • Anonymous analytics (aggregate, non-identifying usage statistics)

Neither integration provides those third parties with any information that could personally identify you.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Economic Area). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us and we will take appropriate steps to remove that information.

GDPR - Your Rights (EEA Users)

If you are located in the European Economic Area (EEA), you have the following rights under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR):

  • Right of access: You may request information about any personal data we hold about you.
  • Right to erasure: You may request deletion of your data. For local data, this is achieved by uninstalling the App.
  • Right to object: You may opt out of personalised advertising through your device settings.
  • Right to lodge a complaint: You have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection supervisory authority.

Because AquaLog collects no personal data beyond anonymous analytics and ad identifiers, most GDPR rights are satisfied by the App's design. To exercise any right or for any GDPR inquiry, please contact us at the address below.

Data Security

Because AquaLog stores all user-generated data locally on your device and collects no personal information on our servers, the security risks normally associated with centralised data storage do not apply. Your device's own security protections (screen lock, encryption) safeguard your local app data.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of the App after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
